FRIDAY Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

FRIDAY is a personal app used only by its owner, Doojin Baek.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, FRIDAY can read and act on his Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Sheets, only for the tasks he asks it to do.

Where data is kept

Google sign-in tokens and any data FRIDAY reads are stored only on the owner's own computer. FRIDAY does not send this data to the developer or to any third party, and does not sell or share it.

Use of Google user data

FRIDAY's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the stored tokens can be deleted from the owner's computer.
